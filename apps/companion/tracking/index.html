Recording guide & limitations

Prepare the camera on the Quest launcher. Under Record a demonstration, choose Record take, then enter AR and perform the interaction. Exit AR to finish, then Save take. Open that .zip here to review the video and tracked poses. Takes are limited to 90 seconds; no audio is recorded.

Existing takes may contain only head poses and still images. Hand joints and continuous video must be captured by the updated recorder. You can also load a tracking .jsonl file and attach a Quest video, then adjust its start offset.

World and POV are coordinate views of the recorded telemetry, not a reconstruction of the room. The RGB video and pose view are shown side by side because passthrough camera calibration is unavailable. This viewer does not estimate poses from ordinary videos or establish training-data accuracy.
